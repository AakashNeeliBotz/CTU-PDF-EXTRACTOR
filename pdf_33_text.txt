Annexure-V”. The same may be corrected as “As per Annexure-IV”.

downsize their application for grant of connectivity at Bhadla-III PS under protest in view of pendency of Writ Petition filed by M/s Avaada before 
Hon’ble High Court of Delhi. 
Other than this, no comments on deliberations held were received on the minutes, accordingly, minutes were confirmed with above corrections.

2200000522(300 MW)). In view of the withdrawal of two applications, for the application of 300 MW at S. No. 1, it was proposed to grant connectivity at 220 kV 
through S/c line. M/s Renew agreed for the same.   
 
Subsequent to mail dated 28.06.2024 confirming withdrawal by M/s Renew, M/s Renew vide mail dated 30.06.2024 again informed that they are not withdrawing 
the applications (2200000521(200 MW) & 2200000522(300 MW)) as informed in the meeting and they agree for the grant of connectivity as originally proposed by 
CTUIL at 400 kV Bhadla-IV PS.  
 
M/s Renew may note that such frequent change in decisions will not be entertained in future and authorized representative should bring their management firm 
decision before any communication in the meeting/mail. M/s Renew vide mail dated 12.07.2024 as well as in the meeting assured that this would not be repeated 
in future. Accordingly, it was decided that the above applications shall be again taken up for stakeholders discussion in the subsequent CMETS NR meeting for 
grant. 
 
Considering the request by M/s Renew to process the above 3 no. of applications, it was proposed to grant connectivity to M/s Renew at 400 kV Bhadla-IV PS 
through 1 no. of bay for the combined connectivity of 800 MW (300+200+300). M/s Renew vide letter dated 30.06.2024 had already confirmed the tower 
configuration as S/c on D/c & 400 kV bay shall be under ISTS. The same is noted.  
 
It was mentioned that the transmission system for evacuation of power from Bhadla-IV is presently under evolution. Upon evolution, the scheme shall be discussed 
in a separate joint study meeting among CEA, CTUIL, Grid India & Stakeholders followed by the subsequent CMETS meeting. The detailed transmission system 
shall be informed upon finalization and approval of the scheme.

It was informed that in the 31st CMETS NR meeting held on 27.06.2024, it was proposed to grant connectivity to NTPC Renewable at Barmer-II PS at 220 kV level 
through S/c line. M/s NTPC informed that they have filed additional applications for about 900 MW in last two months at Fatehgarh-IV PS & requested CTUIL to 
grant connectivity at 400 kV combinedly for all applications. Regarding this, CTUIL in the 31st CMETS NR meeting had clarified that, as the present application is 
for 200 MW, connectivity cannot be granted at 400 kV at this stage. However, when the future applications come up for discussion in the subsequent CMETS 
meetings for grant & if those applications can be accommodated to Barmer-II PS as per application priority, then the connectivity can be granted at 400 kV based 
on total connectivity quantum at Barmer-II PS. Therefore, at that stage connectivity was considered for grant at 220 kV only. NTPC requested to take up all 
applications together in next CMETS meeting for optimization of DTL. After deliberations, it was decided that the above application shall be again taken up for 
discussion in the subsequent CMETS NR meeting along with the other applications of NTPC in Fatehgarh-Barmer complex. 
 
M/s NTPC in the present meeting informed that they have decided to withdraw the present application due to huge mismatch between start date of connectivity at 
Barmer-II PS & PPA/LOA timeline. The same was confirmed by NTPC Renewable vide mail dated 06.08.2024. Accordingly, the application is decided to be closed 
as per Connectivity & GNA Regulations 2022.
